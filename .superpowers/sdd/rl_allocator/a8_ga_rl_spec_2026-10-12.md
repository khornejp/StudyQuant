## 2. Action and policy

Policy action is factorized into three categorical heads:

1. joint target/cadence head, 45 actions: target `{-1,-0.75,-0.5,-0.25,0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1}` times cadence `{5,15,60,240,1440}` minutes;
2. SL head: `{none,1,2,3,5}` times `sigma_RV(wait)`;
3. TP head: `{none,1,2,4,8}` times `sigma_RV(wait)`.

The joint 45 head preserves target/cadence interaction. Separate SL and TP heads avoid a sparse 1,125-way head while retaining every combination. At decision minute `i`, `sigma_RV(wait) = RV24h(i) * sqrt(wait/1440)` in bps, where `RV24h` is the causal square-root sum of the previous 1,440 one-minute log-return squares ending at `i`. `none` stores distance zero.

Every decision replaces the current SL/TP choices. Distances are anchored to the executed position's volume-weighted entry basis, not the current close, so repeated decisions cannot erase accrued adverse/favorable movement. Additions update the basis by executed notional; reductions retain it. SL/TP becomes active on the minute after a new entry fill because one-minute OHLC cannot establish fill/exit intrabar order without tick data.

No separate adverse-move wake trigger. SL/TP already ends the step at the first selected threshold. A 0.5-SL wake would add a fourth action/state interaction without an independent exit or risk constraint.

Policy architecture:

- input: 44 tokens x 20 channels, market channels, and A8 account channels;
- learned token and family embeddings, positive genotype gate multiplication, shared token MLP width `{32,64}`, gated-attention pooling;
- pooled token state plus market/account state into GRU hidden size 256;
- heads: joint 45, SL 5, TP 5, value 1;
- recurrent rollouts span 48 h with 24 h burn-in;
- elapsed-time GAE lambda half-life gene in `{6h,12h,24h}`;
- reward clipping off; advantage normalization on.

## 4. Reward

For every complete or terminal partial five-minute block:

`raw net4 - idle - quadratic inventory risk - target turnover + exit shaping`

- `idle = idle_base/12 * clip(RV/RVref,0.25,4) * (1+slope*min(flat_streak_h,24)) * I(flat)`;
- `risk = risk/12 * clip(RV/RVref,0.25,4)^2 * executed_position^2`;
- `turnover = turnover * abs(new_target-old_target)` on the first block only.
- cap shaping at settlement: `-c_cap * abs(executed position immediately before settlement)` bps;
- TP shaping: `k_tp * max(completed-position PnL, 0)` only when the exit reason is TP;
- loss shaping: `k_loss * min(completed-position PnL, 0)` for every losing exit reason, including SL, cap, RL, and a net-losing TP.

The three coefficients are GA genes. `c_cap` has support `{0} union [0.25,16]` bps. `k_tp` and `k_loss` each have support `{0} union [0.01,0.50]`. Initial sampling assigns probability 0.25 to exact zero, otherwise log-uniform sampling. Mutation preserves exact zero as a reachable mass and mutates nonzero values in log space within bounds.

Shaping is booked in the five-minute reward block containing the exit. The shared `maker_kernel._minute` computes it for scalar and batch execution. Shaping never enters equity, completed-position PnL, raw net4, or GA fitness.

Hacking controls: TP reward is proportional to positive realized PnL, so a narrow TP has no fixed-bonus farming path. `k_loss` applies to all losing exit reasons, so disabling SL or relabeling a loss as cap/RL cannot avoid the penalty. The cap term scales with actual settled exposure. Raw-net4 fitness, maker fees, turnover penalty, and the unchanged ledger reject shaping-only gains. A policy can still reduce or close exposure before cap; this is accepted economic behavior and remains charged by maker cost and turnover.

The final partial block created by SL, TP, cap, data end, or fault is included. Raw net4 remains exact ledger equity change. GA fitness is cumulative raw net4 over 2022-2023.

## 5. SL/TP execution

Each minute monitors the position executed at the start of the minute.

- Long: SL touches when low is at/below basis times `(1-distance)`; TP touches when high is at/above basis times `(1+distance)`. Short is symmetric.
- If SL and TP touch in one minute, SL wins. This is the conservative rule under unknown intrabar order.
- SL fill: trigger price, except an adverse gap through the trigger fills at the minute open, which is worse. TP fill: trigger price with no favorable gap improvement, representing a resting maker order.
- A stop check precedes an ordinary pending-order fill in the same minute. An exit cancels pending and reversal leg 2.
- Exit fee is always 2 bps times absolute executed size. Funding retains the existing pessimistic settlement rule. Ledger identity tolerance remains `1e-8`.
- SL/TP hit immediately ends the current SMDP step, returns `done=False`, and exposes a new observation at that minute close. The next RL action may place a maker order for the next minute. No automatic re-entry.

Metrics: `sl_count`, `tp_count`, completed-position PnL and net4 attributed to SL/TP, plus exit-reason net4 for RL and cap.

## 6. Renewable 24 h exposure lease

Clock starts at the first flat-to-nonflat executed fill minute. That fill minute counts as minute 1. Same-direction increase and partial reduction do not reset it. Executed flat or an actual reversal followed by a new fill starts a new clock.

- Effective wait is `min(RL wait, cap remaining)` for a position already open.
- At processed position minute 1,440: process market PnL and pessimistic funding, cancel pending/leg2, settle at close, charge 2 bps times absolute executed size, set position and coins to zero, and end the SMDP step.
- Cap event uses `done=False`. Next RL decision occurs at that close. A new maker order can rest from the next minute, making decision gap at most one minute.
- Same-direction re-entry is allowed only through the new RL action. No automatic re-entry.
- Next observation includes cap event, previous target, and cumulative renewal cost.

Metrics: `renewal_count`, renewal exit plus first authorized re-entry fees, re-entry request/fill rate, flat gap minutes, maximum executed hold, and cap net4.
